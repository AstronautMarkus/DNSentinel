What is DNSentinnel?

DNSentinnel is a simple yet powerful web application designed with a single, focused purpose: automatically keep your dynamic IP address synchronized with your DNS server, especially when your Internet Service Provider (ISP) frequently changes your IP address.

Perfect for users managing home servers, IoT devices, or remote services that need consistent domain resolution.

The problem with having a dynamic IP address

How it works

DNSentinnel automatically updates your router's IP address in the background, ensuring your DNS always points to the correct IP. This way, your services and websites remain accessible regardless of any IP changes made by your Internet provider.

Benefits

  • Automatic IP address updates
  • Secure: DNSentinel runs only in your local network
  • Reduced downtime due to IP changes
  • Easy setup and configuration
